三、语法梳理 — 频率副词 Adverbs of Frequency

✅ 频率副词的含义与位置

频率副词表示动作发生的频繁程度，通常放在实义动词前面，be动词后面。

频率副词含义百分比例句
always总是100%Lily always escapes.
usually通常~80%Lily is usually very nervous.
often经常~60%Leo often climbs trees.
sometimes有时~30%Leo sometimes tries to catch Lily.
hardly ever几乎不~10%I hardly ever sleep at night.
never从不0%They never get along together.
⚠️ 关键规则

• 实义动词前：Lily always escapes.（always 在 escapes 前）
• be动词后：Lily is usually nervous.（usually 在 is 后）
• never = 0%，hardly ever ≈ 几乎不

5

三、语法梳理 — 对比与例句

📌 频率从高到低

always (100%) → usually (80%) → often (60%) → sometimes (30%) → hardly ever (10%) → never (0%)

📌 放在实义动词前

Lily always escapes.
Leo sometimes tries to catch Lily.
They never get along.

📌 放在be动词后

Lily is usually very nervous.
Leo is often brave.
They are always together.
