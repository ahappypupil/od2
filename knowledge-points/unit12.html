一个果汁盒的日记。它先在商店货架上 → 被小女孩买走 → 被扔到湖里成为垃圾 → 被人从湖里救出 → 到回收工厂 → 变成笔记本在文具店里。

练习 4：阅读理解

January 15: I'm sitting on a shelf in a big store! I can see many wonderful things. The little carton in front of me has a picture of an orange on it. I think I know what I am. I'm an orange juice carton!

January 18: I saw a man taking litter out of the lake with a net. He saw my bright orange carton, and he rescued me! He caught me and a plastic bottle in his net. Now we're in the boat, and we aren't trash anymore. We're happy.

January 20: I'm sitting on a shelf in a stationery store! Everything here is made of paper!

  1. Where was the carton on January 15? → On a ______.
  2. What picture was on the carton? → An ______.
  3. Who rescued the carton? → A ______.
  4. Where was the carton on January 20? → In a ______ ______.
  5. What was everything made of in the stationery store? → ______.
1. shelf (in a big store) 2. orange 3. man 4. stationery store 5. paper
