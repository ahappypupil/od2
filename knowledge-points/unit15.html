💡 力的概念

💪 Force（力）— makes something move
👉 Push（推）— moves something away from you
👈 Pull（拉）— moves something toward you
🏃 Speed（速度）— how fast something moves
🐘 Heavy things need a big force to move.
🪶 Light things need a small force to move.

三、语法梳理 — 比较级形容词

✅ Comparative Adjectives（比较级）

比较两个事物时，用比较级。短形容词加 -er，用 than 连接。

规则原级比较级例句
加 -erlightlighterA ball is lighter than a desk.
heavyheavierIs a desk heavier than a ball?
fastfasterA horse is faster than a tortoise.
slowslowerA train is slower than an airplane.
bigbiggerA horse is bigger than a tortoise.
smallsmallerI'm smaller than a horse.
⚠️ 注意

• 一般加 -er：light → lighter, fast → faster
• 以 -y 结尾变 y 为 i 加 -er：heavy → heavier
• 双写末尾辅音加 -er：big → bigger

练习 4：阅读理解

We see things move every day. We see people walking, birds flying, and leaves falling to the ground on a windy day. Things move in many ways. They move up and down, right and left, and over and under things.

Forces: A force makes something move. A push is a force, and it moves something away from you. We push people on swings. A pull is a force, and it moves something toward you. We pull a wagon.

Speed: A big force makes something move fast. A small force makes something move slowly. Heavy things need a big force to move them. Light things need a small force to move them.

  1. What makes something move? → A ______.
  2. What does a push do? → Moves something ______ from you.
  3. What does a pull do? → Moves something ______ you.
  4. What do heavy things need to move? → A ______ force.
  5. What do light things need to move? → A ______ force.
1. force 2. away 3. toward 4. big 5. small
