💡 Then vs Now

📭 Communication: Long ago → letters (by horse, train, boat) | Now → e-mail, text messages, Internet
📰 News: Long ago → talked to each other, newspapers, radio at 6 o'clock | Now → TV, Internet anytime
✈️ Travel: Long ago → by ship, by horse, walking (slow) | Now → airplane (fast)

三、语法梳理 — 规则动词过去式

✅ 规则动词过去式构成

在动词词尾加 -ed 表示过去发生的事。

规则原形过去式例句
一般加 -edlistenlistenedPeople listened to the radio.
traveltraveledShe traveled on a ship.
以e结尾加-duseusedShe didn't use a car.
arrivearrivedHe arrived in New York.
辅+y→iedcarrycarriedLetters were carried by horse.
✅ 否定句

否定句：didn't + 动词原形

肯定否定
They listened to the radio.They didn't watch the news on TV.
She used letters.She didn't use e-mail.

四、词法知识 — 字母顺序排列

✅ 字母顺序

字典中的单词按字母顺序排列。如果第一个字母相同，看第二个字母，以此类推。

排列：apple → banana → cat → dog
同首字母：boat → bus → boat（b-o 在 b-u 前面）
同两字母：air → aim → ant（第三个字母比较）

练习 5：阅读理解

Today, communication and travel are fast, and it's easy to get news. But a long time ago things were very different.

Communication: Long ago, people used letters to communicate. They mailed letters to friends and families. Letters were carried by horse and cart, and later by train or boat. Today, communication is different and letters are fast.

News: Before newspapers, people talked to each other to get news. Then people printed the news on paper. Later they listened to the radio for news. Today we can get news any time we want.

Travel: Long ago, people traveled by ship, horse, or walking. It was slow. Today we fly in airplanes. It's fast!

  1. How did people communicate long ago? →
  2. How were letters carried? → By ______.
  3. How did people get news before newspapers? → They ______.
  4. How is travel different now? → Now we ______.
  5. Did they watch news on TV long ago? → No, they ______.
1. They used letters. 2. horse and cart / train / boat 3. talked to each other 4. fly in airplanes 5. didn't (They listened to the radio.)
