✅ Verbs, Nouns, Adjectives, and Pronouns

• Verb（动词）— action word: run, jump, clean
• Noun（名词）— person, place, or thing: tree, water, smoke
• Adjective（形容词）— describes a noun: clean, dirty, beautiful
• Pronoun（代词）— takes the place of a noun: he, she, it, they

练习 4：阅读理解

People Need Natural Resources
Living things need clean air to breathe and clean water to drink. Plants need sunlight and land to grow, and we need plants for food. We use the wood from plants and trees to make a lot of things, like paper, some houses, and furniture.

Taking Care of Our Natural Resources
• Use things until you can't use them anymore.
• Don't throw things away. Reuse them. Refill plastic drink bottles. Make toys from old things.
• Reduce the natural resources you use. Take your own bag to the store.

  1. What do living things need to breathe? → ______.
  2. What do plants need to grow? → ______.
  3. What do we use wood for? → To make ______.
  4. What can you do to reduce? → Take ______ ______.
  5. What can you do to reuse? → ______ ______.
1. Clean air 2. Sunlight and land 3. paper, houses, furniture 4. your own bag 5. Refill bottles / Make toys from old things
