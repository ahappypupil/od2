三、语法梳理 — 不规则动词过去式疑问句

✅ Did + 主语 + 动词原形?
疑问句回答
Did Bandar go to a candy store?Yes, he did.
Did he let go of the candy?No, he didn't.
Did he steal cookies?Yes, he did.
⚠️ 关键

• Did 后面用动词原形：Did he go? ✅ (不是 Did he went? ❌)
• didn't 后面也用动词原形：He didn't let go. ✅

练习 5：阅读理解

A monkey called Bandar lived in the woods near a small town. One day, Bandar was bored and decided to go into the town. Bandar went to a bakery and saw thirty beautiful cookies. He stole a cookie and ate it. It was good! He ate more and more cookies! The baker saw Bandar and chased him. "You greedy little monkey!" he cried.

Then Bandar went to the candy store. He saw two big jars of candy. Bandar put his hands in the jars and grabbed lots of candy. But his hands were too full and he couldn't get them out of the jars! He didn't let go of the candy. The candy maker caught him.

  1. Why did Bandar go to the town? → Because he was ______.
  2. What did Bandar steal from the bakery? → ______.
  3. What did the baker call Bandar? → A ______ little monkey.
  4. Why couldn't Bandar get his hands out? → Because his hands were too ______.
  5. Did Bandar let go of the candy? → No, he ______.
1. bored 2. Cookies. 3. greedy 4. full 5. didn't
