三、语法梳理 — was/were 疑问句

✅ 疑问句结构

把 was/were 提到主语前面 → 疑问句

类型结构例句
单数疑问Was + 主语 + ...?Was the freezer door open?
复数疑问Were + 主语 + ...?Were the students happy?
肯定回答Yes, 主语 + was/were.Yes, it was. / Yes, they were.
否定回答No, 主语 + wasn't/weren't.No, it wasn't. / No, they weren't.
⚠️ 关键规则

• 单数用 Was：Was it...? Was he...? Was she...?
• 复数用 Were：Were they...? Were we...? Were you...?
• 回答保持一致：Was it...? → Yes, it was. / Were they...? → Yes, they were.

四、词法知识 — 缩写 Contractions 复习

✅ 什么是缩写？

缩写是两个词合在一起，去掉一个字母，加撇号 (')。

完整形式缩写例句
is + notisn'tWater isn't a gas.
was + notwasn'tIt wasn't a liquid.
are + notaren'tThey aren't soft.
were + notweren'tThe ice pops weren't in the freezer.
it + isit'sIt's steam!
they + arethey'reThey're soft now.
