三、语法梳理 — 物主代词

✅ Possessive Pronouns（物主代词）

物主代词表示"某人的"，放在名词后面或代替名词。

主格形容词性物主代词名词性物主代词
Imymine
youyouryours
hehishis
sheherhers
weourours
theytheirtheirs
⚠️ 区别

• 形容词性物主代词 + 名词：This is my book.
• 名词性物主代词单独使用：This book is mine.
• Those are your things. → Those things are yours.
• Whose book is this? It's hers. (= her book)

练习 5：阅读理解

We follow rules every day. There are rules for things we do at home, at school, and in a lot of other places.

At home: Parents make rules. They ask us to wash our hands and clean up. They make rules about eating healthy food, watching TV, and bed time.

At school: We raise our hands to speak. We take turns. We are polite to our teachers and classmates.

At the zoo: You can't touch or feed the animals. You should never throw things at the animals.

At the pool: Don't run near the water. You can't eat or drink by the pool.

  1. Who makes rules at home? → ______.
  2. What should you do at school to speak? → ______.
  3. What can't you do at the zoo? → ______.
  4. What can't you do by the pool? → ______.
  5. Why do we have rules? → To keep everyone ______.
1. Parents 2. Raise hands / Take turns 3. Touch or feed the animals 4. Run near the water / Eat or drink 5. safe
