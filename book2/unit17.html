三、语法梳理 — 量词

✅ Quantifiers（量词）

量词表示数量的多少。

量词用法例句
a lot of很多（可数/不可数）There are a lot of shapes.
any否定/疑问（可数复数）There aren't any triangles.
any否定/疑问（不可数）There isn't any red paint.
some肯定（不可数）I have some blue paint.
How many多少（可数）How many seals were there?
How much多少（不可数）How much paper was there?
⚠️ 关键

• 可数名词用 How many：How many shapes?
• 不可数名词用 How much：How much paper?
• a lot of 可用于可数和不可数
• any 用于否定和疑问句

练习 4：阅读理解

We can see shapes in people, animals, nature, art, and much more in our world. They are all around us.

Squares and rectangles have four straight sides and four corners. A square has four sides the same length. A rectangle has two sides longer than the other two.

Circles and ovals don't have corners or straight sides. A circle is round. An oval looks like an egg.

George W. Hart made a sculpture from wood. He uses math to help him make his sculptures. A famous painter called Piet Mondrian inspired people to use shapes and straight lines in their art.

  1. Where can we see shapes? → In people, animals, ______, and art.
  2. How many sides does a square have? → ______.
  3. Does a circle have corners? → No, it ______.
  4. What does an oval look like? → An ______.
  5. What did George W. Hart use to make sculptures? → ______.
1. nature 2. Four 3. doesn't 4. egg 5. wood / math
