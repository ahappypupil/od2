三、语法梳理 — 时间介词

✅ Prepositions of Time
介词用法例句
at具体时刻at night, at 9 o'clock
in某段时间in the morning, in the afternoon
on具体日期/星期on Monday, on January 15
⚠️ 关键

• at + 时间点：at night, at 9:00
• in + 时段：in the morning / afternoon / evening
• on + 星期/日期：on Monday, on January 15

练习 4：阅读理解

People all around the world enjoy music. Some music is fast, and some music is slow. Some music is high, and some music is low.

Pedro listens to slow music at night so he can fall asleep. When we listen to music, we feel different things. Slow music can make us feel sleepy, and fast music can make us feel excited. High notes can sound like birds, and low notes can sound like thunder.

Next time you listen to music, draw a picture of what you imagine. Look at it a week later. Does it help you to remember the music?
