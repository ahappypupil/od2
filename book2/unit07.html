一、单词复习 — 数学词汇

English中文English中文
minus sign减号subtraction减法
take away拿走/减去column列
row行test score考试分数
single-digit number一位数double-digit number两位数
left剩余add加
💡 减法概念

When we subtract, we take things away. Then we can see how many things are left.
5 - 3 = 2 → Five take away three, there are two left.

三、语法梳理 — 不规则动词过去式

✅ 不规则动词

有些动词的过去式不加 -ed，需要单独记忆。

原形过去式例句
buyboughtAlma bought five cookies.
givegaveOlivia gave her sister stickers.
eatateHer sister ate four.
gowentHe went to the store.
havehadHis brother had eight.
stealstoleHe stole a cookie.
⚠️ 否定句

否定句用 didn't + 动词原形：
She didn't buy four. (不是 didn't bought ❌)
She didn't give her twenty-six.
