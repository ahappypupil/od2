二、重点句型

… have … to help them …
……有……来帮助它们……
e.g. Fish have gills to help them breathe.（鱼有鳃来帮助它们呼吸。）
Birds have wings to help them fly.
e.g. Birds have wings and feathers to help them fly.
… lay eggs with (hard/soft) shells.
……产有（硬/软）壳的卵。
e.g. Birds lay eggs with hard shells. / Amphibians lay soft eggs in water.
… are part of this group.
……属于这一类。
e.g. An elephant and an orangutan are part of this group. (mammals)
— What's your favorite part? — …
— 你最喜欢的部分是什么？ — …
e.g. — What's your favorite part? — Frogs have wet skin. That's cool!
Eagles have feathers and wings to help them fly. What animal group are they in?
— Eagles are in the bird group. They're in the bird group.

三、语法梳理 — 主格与宾格代词

✅ Subject Pronouns（主格代词）

主格代词做主语，放在动词前面，表示"谁"做了某事。

人称主格例句
第一人称单数II like animals.
第二人称youYou are smart.
第三人称单数he / she / itHe is brave. / It is a fish.
第一人称复数weWe love animals!
第三人称复数theyThey have gills.
✅ Object Pronouns（宾格代词）

宾格代词做宾语，放在动词或介词后面，表示动作的"承受者"。

主格宾格例句
ImeGills help them breathe.
youyouI like you.
hehimThe fox wants to eat him.
sheherBabies drink milk from her.
ititI love it!
weusThey help us.
theythemGills help them breathe in water.

三、语法梳理 — 对比与例句

📌 主格做主语

Fish have gills.
→ They have gills.

Birds have wings.
→ They have wings.

Lily is nervous.
→ She is usually nervous.

📌 宾格做宾语

Gills help fish breathe.
→ Gills help them breathe.

The fox wants to eat Leo.
→ The fox wants to eat him.

Babies drink milk from mother.
→ Babies drink milk from her.

📌 混合练习

Fish have gills. They have gills. Gills help them breathe.

Rabbits are gentle. They are very gentle.

Leo is clever. He is very clever.

⚠️ 关键规则

• 主格放在动词前面（做主语）：They have gills.
• 宾格放在动词或介词后面（做宾语）：Gills help them breathe.
• 用代词代替前面提到过的名词，避免重复：
  Fish → They（不用 It，因为 fish 这里是复数）
  Leo → He / him

练习 7：阅读理解

All animals can breathe and move, but they're different in many amazing ways. Here we look at how they are the same and how they are different. This helps us put them into groups.

Birds have two legs, two wings, and feathers. Most birds can fly. The wings and feathers help them fly and the feathers help keep them warm. Birds lay eggs with hard shells.

Fish have gills to help them breathe in water. Scales all over their bodies help keep them safe from dangerous fish.

Amphibians can live on land and in water. They need wet skin, so they live in wet places. They lay soft eggs in water. They have gills when they're young.

Mammals have fur or hair. Some live on land and some live in water. Mammals don't lay eggs. Their babies drink milk from their mothers. People are mammals too!

  1. What do birds have to help them fly? →
  2. What do fish have to help them breathe? →
  3. Where do amphibians lay their eggs? →
  4. What covers most mammals' bodies? →
  5. What do mammal babies drink? →
  6. Are people mammals? →
1. Wings and feathers. 2. Gills. 3. In water. 4. Fur or hair. 5. Milk (from their mothers). 6. Yes, they are.
