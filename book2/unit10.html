三、语法梳理 — Can and May

✅ Can（能力/请求）

Can 表示"能/可以"，用于询问能力或请求许可。

类型例句
请求Can I play? Yes, you can.
能力I can swim.
✅ May（更礼貌的请求）

May 比 can 更正式、更礼貌，用于请求许可。

类型例句
礼貌请求May I come in? No, you may not.

四、词法知识 — 主谓一致

✅ Subject-Verb Agreement（主谓一致）

写句子时，确保动词形式与主语一致。

主语be动词例句
He (单数)isHe is careful with scissors and glue.
They (复数)are/wereThey were tired yesterday.
She (单数)is/wasShe is polite.

练习 4：阅读理解

The Ox Always Knocks
The ox always knocks before opening a door, because someone behind it might be sleeping or wrapping a present or sad or dreaming. Then he always asks, "May I come in?" And everyone says, "How thoughtful of him!"

  1. What does the ox always do before opening a door? → He ______.
  2. Why does the ox knock? → Because someone behind it might be ______.
  3. What does the ox always ask? → "______ I come in?"
  4. What do people say about the ox? → "How ______ of him!"
  5. What is the theme of this poem? → It's good to think about ______ before we do something.
1. knocks 2. sleeping/sad/dreaming 3. May 4. thoughtful 5. other people
