三、语法梳理 — 量词疑问句

✅ How many / How much
疑问词用法例句
How many可数名词复数How many seals were there?
How much不可数名词How much paper was there?
✅ 回答
问题回答
How many seals? →There was one seal. / There were three seals.
How much paper? →There was a lot of paper. / There wasn't any paper.
Did Maki have any pencils? →No, she didn't.

四、词法知识 — 逗号分隔列表

✅ Using Commas in Lists（逗号分隔列表）

用逗号分隔句子中三个或更多的项目。

无逗号有逗号
You can take a bus a train or an airplane.You can take a bus, a train, or an airplane.
My sister bought glue, a box of markers, colored pencils, scissors, and chalk.
⚠️ 规则

• 在每个项目之间加逗号
• 最后一个项目前加 and
• and 前面也可以加逗号（牛津逗号）

练习 4：阅读理解

Maki woke up early. Her room was quiet. Everyone was sleeping. What could she do? "I know," she thought. "I can do origami!" On the table was some colored paper. The paper was shiny, and there were a lot of colors.

Maki folded a red square, bending the paper in the middle and at the edges. She worked until it was a beautiful crane. With blue paper, Maki made water, crumpling it to make waves. Maki dived into the water and swam happily with the cranes and seal.

But something was missing. Maki remembered her sister, Akiko. She missed her and wanted to see her. Maki saw a sailboat. "All aboard!" It was Akiko!

  1. What did Maki do when she woke up early? → She did ______.
  2. What was the paper like? → It was ______.
  3. What did Maki make with red paper? → A ______.
  4. What did she make with blue paper? → ______.
  5. Who came on the sailboat? → ______.
1. origami 2. shiny 3. crane 4. water (waves) 5. Akiko (her sister)
