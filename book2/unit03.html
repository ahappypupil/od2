一、单词复习 — 物质三态与变化

English中文English中文
flow流动solid固体
liquid液体gas气体
heat加热/热量steam蒸汽
ice冰freeze结冰
melt融化boil沸腾
💡 物质三态

🔵 Solid（固体）— can see and feel, hard or soft (trees, feathers, you!)
🟡 Liquid（液体）— can flow, thick or thin (juice, milkshakes)
🔴 Gas（气体）— can't always see, fills space (steam, air)

🔄 变化：Water freezes → ice (liquid → solid) | Ice melts → water (solid → liquid) | Water boils → steam (liquid → gas)

三、语法梳理 — 动词 to be 的一般过去时

✅ was / were 的用法

用 was/were 表示过去的状态（"是/在"）。

主语be动词过去时例句
IwasI was happy yesterday.
He / She / ItwasIt was a liquid. Now it's a gas.
You / We / TheywereThey were hard. Now they're soft.
✅ 否定句与疑问句

否定句：在 was/were 后加 not → wasn't / weren't
疑问句：把 was/were 提前

类型例句
否定It wasn't a gas. / They weren't soft.
疑问Was it a liquid? Yes, it was. / Were they hard? No, they weren't.
5

三、语法梳理 — 对比与例句

📌 过去 → 现在

The snow was a solid and now it's a liquid.
The water was a liquid and now it's a solid.
The water was a liquid and now it's a gas.

📌 was vs were

单数 → was: It was, He was, She was
复数 → were: They were, We were, You were

📌 否定与疑问

It wasn't hot.
Was it cold? Yes, it was.
They weren't hard.
Were they soft? No, they weren't.

四、词法知识 — 名词与动词

✅ 同一个词可做名词也可做动词

有些词既可做名词又可做动词，需根据上下文判断。

单词词性例句
steam名词Steam is a gas. (蒸汽是气体。)
steam动词She steams the carrots. (她蒸胡萝卜。)
water名词Water is a liquid.
water动词I water the plants.
heat名词The heat melts the ice.
heat动词We heat the water.

练习 5：阅读理解

Everything in our world comes in three different states: solid, liquid, or gas.

Solids: We can see and feel solids. Some solids are hard, and some solids are soft. Trees and feathers are solids. You're a solid, too!

Liquids: A liquid is a thing that can flow. Some liquids are thick, and some liquids are thin. Juice and milkshakes are liquids.

Gases: You can't always see gases, but they are all around us! Steam is a gas. Air is a gas, too.

Things can change from one state to another when we make them hot or cold. The snow was a solid and now it's a liquid. The water was a liquid and now it's a solid. The water was a liquid and now it's a gas.
